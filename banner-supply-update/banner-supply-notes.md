# Banner 2: warehouse loading scene

The original orange truck, orange container and white palletized bags are preserved. Transparent native IMOKO logos sit on the vehicle and container, filtered to off-white (#f5f5f5). Labels are attached to each moving object's wrapper.

The warehouse is larger (86% of the illustration stage, previously 72%). Only its sprite image is mirrored so the doorway faces the truck's rear. Its unmirrored sign reads:

Imoko warehouse

Plot 8, Block H, Cowbell Road, Isolo, Industrial estate, Isolo-Lagos.

The same full address is included in the artwork's accessible description. Each visible stack receives eight small dark-ink labels reading NIMIR / PLAM BRIGHT, preserving the user's supplied spelling and the original white bags, stack arrangement and wooden pallet.

The six-second sequence holds the warehouse, container and stock stack still. The truck first moves down-left to leave a loading gap, holds from 18% through 76% of the cycle while a second matching pallet leaves the doorway, rises to bed height and passes behind the truck's rear, then pulls forward. The end of the loop briefly fades and resets the moving objects. Pallet motion and its labels share one wrapper and sit below the truck's stacking layer so the load is occluded by the truck instead of sliding over its side.

The animation runs only while banner 2 is active and honors the existing pause, page visibility and reduced-motion controls. Reduced-motion mode shows the truck in its forward position with the stationary labeled stock stack; the duplicate loading pallet is hidden. All scene positions remain relative to the existing illustration stage and preserve the shared banner dimensions. Other banner compositions are unchanged.
