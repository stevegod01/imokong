# Banner 4 supplied Nicnax products

Source: user-supplied `C:/Users/ejiog/Downloads/almond granola.jpg`.

The website uses the original photograph unchanged, with a native SVG alpha mask around the product silhouettes. This retains the photographed packaging, printed labels and white areas of each pack while hiding the white backdrop, floor shadows and incomplete standalone wordmark at the bottom. The source JPG itself remains opaque; transparency is applied by its matching mask.

One built-in Imagegen background-extraction attempt was made. It produced an opaque RGB checkerboard and changed packaging typography. That candidate and its prompt are retained in the staging asset folder for provenance and are not used on the site.

The product group occupies the existing banner artwork area and gently floats with the shared pause/reduced-motion controls. Banner 4's food-grain sketch underlay is unchanged. The phone layout uses available horizontal space within the existing artwork-height budget.
