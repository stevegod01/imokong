White packaging and label areas remain opaque inside the product shapes; all surrounding backdrop pixels are hidden.

Imagegen redrew some typography and framing, so this is a stylized banner illustration rather than a pixel-identical photograph. It should not be used as authoritative packaging specifications.

The existing banner 4 sketch background, text, artwork area and six-second gentle float remain unchanged. The photo and its mask both use centered `contain` sizing so their edges stay aligned on phones and desktops.
