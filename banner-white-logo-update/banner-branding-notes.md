# Banner 1 branding

Only the company introduction slide uses the added branding. The existing transparent IMOKO logo sits directly on the truck's orange side panel and the chemical drum's metal body, with no background plates or shadows. The truck logo is rendered in off-white (approximately #f5f5f5) with a CSS filter that preserves its transparency. Each logo lives inside the same animated wrapper as its object, so it follows every movement.

For banner 1, `public/images/banner-steel-drum.png` replaces the drum with a matching unpainted stainless-steel version. The orange band is removed from the artwork, and the original IMOKO logo is overlaid directly on its clean middle surface. The industrial-materials banner retains its separate original artwork. The exact built-in Imagegen edit prompt is saved as `steel-drum-prompt.txt`.

The Capella bag has been removed from the banner's artwork registry, item list, preload list, accessible description and animation styles. Its original source asset and extraction notes remain available as design references.

All banners retain their shared viewport sizing and six-second rotation interval. The refinery sketch underlay remains on banner 1.
