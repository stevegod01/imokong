Who are we?

An Industrial chemical distribution company in Nigeria

One of the top industrial chemical distribution companies in Nigeria. We import and support industries with raw materials such as soap noodles of different grades and quality, glycerine, stearic acid, and other industrial chemicals.

Contact Us

We follow a set of policies, codes, and standards.

IMOKO imports and distributes premium raw materials (soap noodles, solvents, glycerine, stearic acid, and other industrial chemicals) for industrial manufacturers, and also supplies finished food products such as oatmeal, granola, and consumer goods. We serve manufacturers, retailers, and food service providers across Nigeria with verified quality and competitive pricing.
